# Changelog

## v1.1.0 (2026-08-20)

1. Meeting controls moved into a navbar fixed to the top of the page; the table header is gone.
1. The last speaker is now tracked across pauses and mayhems, highlighted in the roster, and shown as the navbar pause/resume button with their running time.
1. Previous/next speaker buttons that skip absent members; advancing past the last speaker pauses the meeting.
1. Keyboard shortcuts: J (previous), K (pause/resume), L (next), M (mayhem), with key hints visible on the buttons.
1. The active speaker's row is automatically scrolled into view when off-screen.
1. All durations render as addons attached to their buttons, with tabular digits so they don't wobble while ticking.

## v1.0.0 (2026-08-19)

Initial release: team roster from `config.js`, presence checkboxes, seeded daily shuffle with pinned members, per-speaker timers, mayhem mode, and meeting charts.
